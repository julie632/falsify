A small experiment, a precise question

What this prototype can establish

We compare an evaluation with shared participants against a control that holds participants apart. The lab checks the relationship between the stated claim, the data split, and the measured result.

Two cases demonstrate feasibility. They do not establish general agent reliability. A flawed evaluation does not prove that the classifier is bad; it means the claim needs appropriate evidence.
